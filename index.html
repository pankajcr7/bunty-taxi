03 — The person behind it

Started with
one car,
one promise.

In 2014 Bunty was driving a single white sedan out of a rented parking spot in West Delhi. The pitch was not clever — show up on time, keep the car clean, charge what you said you would. Eleven years later that is still the whole operating manual.

Today the same rule covers trips to fifty-odd cities, and a dispatch line that runs through the night. The car changed. The rule did not.

Anyone can put a car on the road. The hard part is answering the phone at 3 AM and being there twenty minutes later. That is the part I refuse to get wrong.
— Bunty
